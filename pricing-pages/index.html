Designing a pricing experience that scales with the customer

Scope UI Design · 3 Phases

The Subscription & Billing page went through three distinct design phases — each one giving users more control and removing friction from the path to purchase. Here's how it evolved.

Phase 1
Sales-led

Static plan, hand-off to sales

The first version showed a single fixed plan — $4,170/year — with a clearly defined feature set. The "Purchase a plan" button handed users off to the sales team to complete the transaction.

The page did a good job communicating value: trial usage meters at the top, a feature checklist, add-on cards, and an FAQ. But the conversion path required human intervention at the critical moment.

Key constraint: No self-serve path. Every purchase required a sales touchpoint.

Subscription & Billing — Phase 1
Phase 2
Self-serve

Inline checkout modal

Phase 2 brought the entire purchase flow in-app. Clicking "Purchase a plan" now opens a modal with a full payment form — card details, coupon code, and a live order summary.

The plan and price remained fixed, but users could now complete the transaction without ever leaving the product or waiting for a sales rep.

What changed: Removed the sales hand-off entirely. Users can now go from trial to paid in one sitting.

Subscription & Billing — Phase 2 · Checkout modal
Phase 3
Customisable

Right-size your plan

Phase 3 gave users direct control over what they were buying. Two sliders — Destination Clusters and ArgoCD Applications — let users build their own plan, with the total updating in real time.

Three preset bundles (Startup, Growing Company, Multi-Department) provided quick starting points for users who weren't sure where to begin. An Annual/Monthly toggle made the 20% discount tangible rather than buried in small print.

The purchase flow became a deliberate two-step process: Plan Setup → Checkout, making it clear what users were committing to before they entered payment details.

What changed: Moved from fixed pricing to dynamic pricing. Users now build a plan that fits their scale — and can see exactly what they're getting before they pay.
